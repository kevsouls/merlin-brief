# NPS thesis: Merlin and AFSOC defensive maneuvering

Added 2026-10-04 from a page Kevin sent. This is a student research collaboration, not a contract award.

## What is public

Maj Randy S. Buckley (USAF) and Maj Jonathan D. Pryor (USAF) wrote a Naval Postgraduate School master's thesis, submitted June 2022:

**Autonomous Maneuvering: A Defense Advantage for AFSOC Aircraft**

- Archive: [Calhoun handle 10945/70640](https://hdl.handle.net/10945/70640)
- PDF: [NPS bitstream](https://calhoun.nps.edu/server/api/core/bitstreams/07b7490d-7194-4abb-a623-642e98acf2a2/content)
- Degrees: MS in Defense Analysis (Irregular Warfare) and MS in Applied Design for Innovation

The abstract says the thesis looks at replicating Merlin Labs' approach to flight automation and putting a machine-trained system that can fly defensive maneuvers onto existing aircraft, to cut reaction time against surface-to-air threats. The executive summary says the work was done in collaboration with Merlin Labs, described there as a Boston flight-automation startup. The recommendation is more defensive capability on SOF aircraft and onboard autonomy on platforms that are still flown by people. It also calls for more research on optionally manned SOF aircraft.

## How the department page differs

The page Kevin sent is a Defense Analysis department summary, not the thesis. The same paragraph is in [this PDF](https://mccareer.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/defense-analysis-2023-feb-c-web.pdf) (filename dates it February 2023).

That summary shortens the title to "Autonomous Maneuvering: An Advantage for AFSOC Aircraft" and dates the work to 2021. The thesis itself is June 2022. Use the thesis title and date when citing the research. Use the department page only for the follow-on line.

As of that writeup, two students, Maj William Borges (USAF) and MSgt Daniel Christenson (USAF), were designing a thesis on the next steps. That line does not say the follow-on was finished or funded.
