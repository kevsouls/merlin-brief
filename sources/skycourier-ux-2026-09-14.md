# Cessna SkyCourier UX, 14 September 2026

Textron Aviation and Merlin introduced a concept, not a flying demonstrator and not a program of record.

[Textron release](https://investor.textron.com/news-releases/news-details/2026/Textron-Aviation-and-Merlin-Introduce-Cessna-SkyCourier-UX-Concept-Aircraft/default.aspx). Revealed at the Air, Space and Cyber Conference, National Harbor. The concept pairs the FAA-certified Cessna SkyCourier with Merlin Pilot for contested logistics. Textron says it is evaluating mission fit, integration, safety, and customer interest.

[Textron's own Q&A](https://media.txtav.com/270917-cessna-skycourier-ux-what-to-know-about-the-autonomous-logistics-concept/) says the SkyCourier UX is not a flying demonstrator or a program of record. Payload figure they give for the existing SkyCourier is up to 6,000 pounds and three LD3 containers.

Merlin's [writeup](https://merlinlabs.com/news/meet-the-skycourier-ux-from-textron-aviation-and-merlin/) adds two claims of its own. In July, a Merlin Pilot on a Cessna 208B landed autonomously at Oshkosh. In August, Merlin reached Stage of Involvement 3 with New Zealand's Civil Aviation Authority on the flight-control computer and the automated communication system, with the FAA under a bilateral agreement. Those are Merlin's sentences, not Textron's.
