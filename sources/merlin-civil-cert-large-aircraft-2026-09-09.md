2. CEO note by Matt George:  
   https://merlinlabs.com/news/moving-our-civil-certification-strategy-to-large-aircraft/  
   - Date: September 09, 2026  
   - States Merlin started a small aircraft certification program in New Zealand five years ago.  
   - States the team achieved Stage of Involvement (SOI) 3 and a Stage 4 issue paper addressing AI/ML-based natural language processing with the New Zealand Civil Aviation Authority (CAANZ).  
   - States Merlin is shutting that small aircraft program down.  
   - States Merlin is concentrating civil certification investment on large aircraft.  
   - States Kerikeri remains Merlin's flight test center and Pacific demonstration home.  
   - States Merlin intends to begin commercial operations there under the CAANZ Part 107 rule.  
   - Frames the decision as where to invest capital and resources (return on finalizing small-aircraft certification vs large aircraft), not as a regulator rejection.

## Desk implication

Home-page copy that treated dual-track FAA + New Zealand CAA civil certification as Merlin's current moat / all-clear break condition is outdated as of this 9 September 2026 strategy change.
