# Timeline

Dates that sit near Merlin, including ones that are not Merlin. Each line says which.

**Merlin** means a Merlin source names it. **Nearby** means the same customer or aircraft, and Merlin is not named. A planned date is not a result.

## Merlin

- **2019.** Precursor autonomy work on a Twin Otter, as told to Inside Unmanned Systems. [Interview, 8 July 2026](sources/inside-unmanned-systems-2026-07-08.md).
- **2020.** SOCOM and AFSOC wrote a concept of employment for a remotely piloted AC-130J and selected Merlin for basic flight operations. That is the authors' account in a student thesis, not the concept document. [Buckley and Pryor, June 2022](sources/nps-afsoc-autonomous-maneuvering-2022.md).
- **June 2022.** NPS thesis on defensive maneuvering with Merlin. Collaboration and a planned C-130J schedule through December 2022. Not a fleet install. Same note.
- **4 June 2024.** Merlin press release: checklists, dynamic rerouting, and emergency landing were skills in development. No aircraft or customer on the page. Not added as a source note. Business Wire.
- **February 2026.** Matt George's shareholder letter, as quoted by the magazine: sole prime on SOCOM's C-130J autonomy program, milestones, and a path to fleet-wide deployment. Path is not a funded fleet buy. [Interview note](sources/inside-unmanned-systems-2026-07-08.md).
- **4 June 2026.** Merlin announced it completed the critical design review on the C-130J autonomy program with SOCOM, and said the next steps are integration, ground test, then flight demos. Company release. The July magazine article does not mention this review.
- **8 July 2026.** SOF Week interview. Autonomy on the C-130J and KC-135 fleets already flying. Nine-figure production contract is the IDIQ ceiling, not money spent. [Interview note](sources/inside-unmanned-systems-2026-07-08.md).

## Nearby, Merlin not named

- **2019.** Project Virtue starts: agile prototype sprints for SOF C-130J Integrated Tactical Mission Systems. Mission software and data links, not a Merlin pilot. May 2023 SOCOM brief, Lt Col Kevin McClure, PEO Fixed Wing.
- **July and November, year not on the slide.** ITMS demos on an MC-130J: route replanner, simulator in Marietta, then a flight. Lockheed's plant. Not Merlin.
- **May 2023.** SOCOM brief, "Modernizing Acquisition Processes for Agile Capability Delivery," ITMS requirements. Not Merlin.
- **April 2026.** SOCOM PB2027 schedule, program element 1160403BB, project SF100. Row "Autonomy / Reduced Workload" runs through fiscal 2030. Next green mark is "Mission Autonomy Increment" in fiscal 2027, then "Automated Crew Workload." No company named. Exhibit R-4, Volume 5, page 64.
